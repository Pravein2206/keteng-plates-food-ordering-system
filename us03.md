## User Story
As a customer, I want to choose an open delivery batch and enter my delivery location so staff receive correct instructions.

## Epic
E1-customer-ordering

## Priority
must

## Story Points
3

## Evidence ID
OBS01, INT01

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Customer can choose one of four configured delivery batches and enter phone, name and address.
- [ ] Missing phone/address or an expired batch prevents submission and shows the field/batch error.
- [ ] At checkout, chosen batch, address and calculated delivery total are shown for review.

## Dependencies / Risks
- Cut-off windows and date/time handling must be verified with owner.
- Use the Nov 03 form as the starting sample, not a permanent schedule.
