## User Story
As a customer, I want to set quantities and see item totals plus the RM2 delivery charge so I know what to pay.

## Epic
E1-customer-ordering

## Priority
must

## Story Points
3

## Evidence ID
OBS01

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Customers can add an available item and increase/decrease its quantity; the cart updates.
- [ ] Total = sum(price × quantity) + RM2 exactly once for a delivery order; an example RM15 + RM8 + RM2 shows RM25.
- [ ] A zero/invalid quantity is rejected and an empty cart cannot proceed to checkout.

## Dependencies / Risks
- Final policy for any non-delivery orders/add-on pricing requires owner confirmation.
- Sprint 1 assumes a delivery-only workflow.
