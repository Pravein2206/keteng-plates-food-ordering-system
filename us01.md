## User Story
As a customer, I want to see a current menu with prices and sold-out labels so I can choose available food.

## Epic
E1-customer-ordering

## Priority
must

## Story Points
3

## Evidence ID
OBS01, INT01

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] A guest customer on a phone can view each seeded product name, price and availability.
- [ ] Sold-out items display “Sold out” and cannot be added to an order.
- [ ] If the menu cannot load, a clear retry/error message appears rather than a blank page.

## Dependencies / Risks
- Needs owner-approved sample item prices/photos.
- Use anonymised/synthetic test products until cleared.
